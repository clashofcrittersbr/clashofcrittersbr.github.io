## Drilleroo · T1 {#drilleroo}

![Drilleroo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jeremycanlas/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/ec83738f3790c306e6e04d8b0878bdf388bd26e8/data/images/tatari/drilleroo.webp){ width=160 height=160 loading=lazy }

**Elemento:** Pedra · **Função:** Especialista · **Raridade:** Rara

### Habilidade normal

Whirl Claw: Swipes claw to deal AoE damage. Sometimes launches a heavy attack that inflicts Knockback on enemies to force them into adjacent rows, or inflicts Shredded on enemies that cannot be knocked back.

### Horda: níveis da partida

Os níveis 3, 5 e 7 abaixo são melhorias durante a Horda, independentes do grau T1–T4.

- **Nível 3 — Shredding Stab:** Attacks have a chance to launch an extra stab forward, dealing AoE damage and inflicting DEF Down. Gains DEF Boost based on the number of enemies hit.
- **Nível 5 — Crowd Crusher:** Periodically gains a Sunder Shield. Value of the shield is based on the number of enemies nearby. The more enemies, the higher the shield value.
- **Nível 7 — Where To?:** Able to move above the deployment area. Periodically gains Prepared. After moving, expends Prepared to deal large AoE damage. When in Danger, retreats and recovers HP while losing this skill's effect for some time.

## Evolução e provas

Os nomes e graus acima descrevem a linhagem. Custos e tarefas não constam desta base e precisam ser conferidos no jogo. O patch de 04/11 substitui provas “Fishing Contest ou Zobo Shooter” por “Fishing Contest ou Artifact Adventure”, e “Raft Race ou Fishing Contest” por “Raft Race ou Zobo Shooter”, com transferência proporcional do progresso. Não há lista de famílias afetadas no anúncio.
