## Funglet · T1 {#funglet}

![Funglet](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jeremycanlas/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/ec83738f3790c306e6e04d8b0878bdf388bd26e8/data/images/tatari/funglet.webp){ width=160 height=160 loading=lazy }

**Elemento:** Planta · **Função:** Suporte · **Raridade:** Rara

### Habilidade normal

Buzzed Ring: Emits a ring of spores that expands, continuously dealing damage to enemies in range. Deals a small amount of damage to allies while granting them ATK and SPD Boost.

### Horda: níveis da partida

Os níveis 3, 5 e 7 abaixo são melhorias durante a Horda, independentes do grau T1–T4.

- **Nível 3 — Weakening Spore:** Launches 1 weakening spore, with a chance to inflict Fragile.
- **Nível 5 — Exploding Spores:** When an enemy dies nearby, triggers an explosion that deals small AoE damage. If the enemy was killed by this Tatari, the explosion deals large AoE damage.
- **Nível 7 — Longevity Spores:** Spreads longevity spores that continuously deal AoE damage.

[Página de origem e histórico de autores](https://clashofcritters.wiki.gg/wiki/Funglet)

## Ecstamira · T4 {#ecstamira}

![Ecstamira](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jeremycanlas/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/ec83738f3790c306e6e04d8b0878bdf388bd26e8/data/images/tatari/ecstamira.webp){ width=160 height=160 loading=lazy }

**Elemento:** Planta · **Função:** Suporte · **Raridade:** Rara

### Habilidade normal

Faded Ring: Emits spreading spores that continuously deal AoE damage and inflict Spore: Fragile. Deals a small amount of damage to allies in range, but also grants them Spore: ATK Speed Boost and Spore: DMG Reduction. Esctamira carries Aura: Grass whenever on the field.

### Horda: níveis da partida

Os níveis 3, 5 e 7 abaixo são melhorias durante a Horda, independentes do grau T1–T4.

- **Nível 3 — Weakening Spore:** Launches 1 weakening spore, with a chance to inflict Fragile.
- **Nível 5 — Exploding Spores:** When an enemy dies nearby, triggers an explosion that deals small AoE damage. If the enemy was killed by this Tatari, the explosion deals large AoE damage.
- **Nível 7 — Longevity Spores:** Spreads longevity spores that continuously deal AoE damage.
