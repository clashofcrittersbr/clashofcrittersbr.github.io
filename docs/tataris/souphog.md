## Souphog · T1 {#souphog}

![Souphog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jeremycanlas/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/ec83738f3790c306e6e04d8b0878bdf388bd26e8/data/images/tatari/souphog.webp){ width=160 height=160 loading=lazy }

**Elemento:** Fogo · **Função:** Curador · **Raridade:** Rara

### Habilidade normal

Spicy Soup: Spends HP to hurl peppers and deals AoE damage based on the amount consumed. Grants allies a Culinary Shield. Heals nearby allies when knocked down.

### Horda: níveis da partida

Os níveis 3, 5 e 7 abaixo são melhorias durante a Horda, independentes do grau T1–T4.

- **Nível 3 — Second Helping:** Has a chance to launch food whenever HP changes.
- **Nível 5 — Free Snacks:** Periodically grants a Snack Shield to allies within a wide range, while also recovering HP.
- **Nível 7 — Food Fight!:** Continuously hurls food at random targets. For enemies, deals damage or inflicts Stun. For allies, recovers HP or grants ATK Boost.

## Searhog · T4 {#searhog}

![Searhog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jeremycanlas/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/ec83738f3790c306e6e04d8b0878bdf388bd26e8/data/images/tatari/searhog.webp){ width=160 height=160 loading=lazy }

Calorie Bomb: Consumes HP to hurl seared meat and deals AoE damage based on the amount consumed. Grants allies ATK Boost and a BBQ Shield that deals damage over time. Heals nearby allies when knocked down. Searhog carries Aura: Fire whenever on the field.

## Evolução e provas

Os nomes e graus acima descrevem a linhagem. Custos e tarefas não constam desta base e precisam ser conferidos no jogo. O patch de 04/11 substitui provas “Fishing Contest ou Zobo Shooter” por “Fishing Contest ou Artifact Adventure”, e “Raft Race ou Fishing Contest” por “Raft Race ou Zobo Shooter”, com transferência proporcional do progresso. Não há lista de famílias afetadas no anúncio.
